# Chapter 1: AI Safety Overview

No code in this chapter.

Chapter 1 is a conceptual map of the AI safety field: what "AI safety" actually covers, immediate model harms, safety vs. refusal, misuse vs. misalignment, alignment as a chain of approximations, Goodhart's Law and reward hacking, the base-model-to-assistant pipeline (SFT, RLHF), distribution shift, jailbreaks vs. prompt injection, agentic risk, dangerous capabilities, frontier/catastrophic risk, scalable oversight, and why AI safety is fundamentally a measurement problem. It sets up the empirical approach that the rest of the book, and this repo, follows.

The hands-on work starts in [Chapter 2](../Chapter%202).
