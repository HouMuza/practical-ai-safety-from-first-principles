# Puro-2B Research Proposal

## Provisional research programme

Investigate how pretraining stage, data composition, curriculum ordering, checkpoint averaging, and deployment choices affect the safety behaviour of Puro-2B.

## First research question

Do Puro-2B models trained with different Phase 2 curricula develop materially different safety profiles despite having similar capability?

## Initial contribution

The study will use Puro's open training artefacts to test whether capability-equivalent pretraining recipes are also safety-equivalent. The first milestone is a reproducible pilot, not a full-scale training run.

## Work stages

1. Review related research and document the gap.
2. Inventory available Puro models, checkpoints, manifests, licences, and reported results.
3. Reproduce a small part of the published capability evaluation.
4. Validate a small safety-evaluation pipeline.
5. Run the checkpoint-safety pilot.
6. Decide whether the evidence supports a larger observational or controlled continued-pretraining study.
